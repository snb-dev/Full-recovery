To look for lost photos and videos, Full Recovery needs permission to read all files on this phone. Nothing is uploaded or changed.

Where did you lose your files?

Choose a drive, memory card or USB stick. Full Recovery only reads it — nothing is ever written to it.

Tip: stop using the drive until your files are back. Saving anything new on it can overwrite the files you want to recover.
